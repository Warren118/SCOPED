# Responsible use

SCOPED-Hiring is a research instrument for diagnosing fairness risks in
simulated LLM-based multi-agent decision trajectories. It does not validate a
system for employment use. The authors request that it not be used to screen,
rank, hire, compensate, promote, or otherwise make consequential decisions
about people.

The controlled signals include identity-related, socioeconomic, geographic,
educational, and employment-trajectory cues. They exist only to test for
differential treatment in matched audit variants; they are not recommended
features for a decision system.

The release is designed to support research verification. Any real-world use
would require independent legal review, human oversight, domain-specific
validation, and protections for affected people.

This document is responsible-use guidance, not an additional legal restriction
on the Apache-2.0 license.
